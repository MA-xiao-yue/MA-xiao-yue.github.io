About Me

I am currently a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong, working with Prof. Cong Wang, with whom I have worked since joining CityUHK as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from George Mason University in 2026, my Master of Information Technology from the University of Melbourne in 2020, and my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Northeastern University in 2017.

My research focuses on system security and trustworthy AIoT systems. I develop automated approaches to vulnerability discovery, protocol fuzzing, and LLM-assisted testing. My interests also include interaction-centric validation and agent/digital twinning for reliable AIoT agents.
